1. About KBTCOE Connect

Established by the historic MVP Samaj (founded in 1914), KBTCOE stands as a pioneer technical institution in North Maharashtra. KBTCOE Connect brings together campus innovation, verified student credentials, technical blogs, and active industry hiring into one collaborative environment.

Through this portal, engineering students can:

  • Maintain a verifiable digital resume highlighting coursework, semester SGPA, and technical projects.
  • Publish technical blogs, research summaries, and competitive programming insights.
  • Form multidisciplinary project squads for national hackathons such as Smart India Hackathon (SIH).
  • Receive direct notifications from the college T&P cell regarding campus recruitment drives and summer internships.

Notice: All submissions are moderated to uphold institutional academic integrity.
